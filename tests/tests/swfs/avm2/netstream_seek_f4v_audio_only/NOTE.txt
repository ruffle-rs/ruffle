test.swf and Test.as are copied from visual/video/f4v.

test.mp4 is the audio track of visual/video/f4v/test.mp4, padded with silence
to 6 seconds (the length of the video track there), so that the stream doesn't
end before the seeks. It was made with:

ffmpeg -i ../../visual/video/f4v/test.mp4 -map 0:a -af apad=whole_dur=6 \
  -c:a aac -b:a 128k -movflags +faststart test.mp4
